Engagement & Help Delivery

AI orchestrates outreach, meetings, synthesis, and reciprocity — ensuring the user actually receives help.

16, 22 AI Outreach & Time Protection

User clicks “Ask AI to reach out”. AI:

  • Contacts a shortlist of matched experts with a standardized request (Story 16)
  • Shows outreach status (Pending → Viewed → Responded) (Story 16)
  • Respects weekly request limits and availability settings (Story 22)

Ongoing Network Visualization

User continuously understands and manages their professional graph via the Network Map and its AI insights.

23–26, 28–33 Interactive Graph & World Map Modes
Graph Mode Filters · Search

Node brightness, edge thickness, and heat overlays communicate recency, intensity, and activity clusters (Stories 23, 25, 32).

World Map Mode Activity Heatmap
World silhouettes & clusters

Toggle between graph and geographic view, search for people/orgs, filter by connection type, and expand second-degree nodes (Stories 24, 26, 28–31, 33).

27, 29–31 Act on the Map
  • Open side panel to message, request help, or schedule directly from nodes (Story 27)
  • Search bar highlights nodes and centers on results (Story 29)
  • Filter by connection type / organizations, with distinct shapes for companies & orgs (Stories 30–31)
32–35 Activity, Suggestions & Guest Mode
  • Activity heatmap reveals “hot” collaboration clusters (Story 32)
  • AI suggests people/orgs to connect with, based on skills and reciprocity (Story 33)
  • Guest users can zoom/hover but must request sponsorship to act (Story 34)
  • AI highlights people to re-engage with based on history (Story 35)
